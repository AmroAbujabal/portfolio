\cvproject{\href{https://github.com/AmroAbujabal/resnet-cifar-repro}{Reproducing ResNet --- Residual Learning on CIFAR}}{Deep Learning · Paper Reproduction}{2026}
\begin{cvbullets}
  \item Rebuilt the CIFAR-10 experiments from He et al. (2015) using only the paper --- model, data
        pipeline and training loop --- test-first (TDD), with tests asserting exact layer counts and
        parameter budgets (0.27M at ResNet-20, 0.85M at ResNet-56) before any training step.
  \item Reproduced ResNet-20 at 8.39 ± 0.31\% and ResNet-56 at 7.45 ± 0.69\% error over 3 seeds,
        both inside the ±0.5\% tolerance fixed before the first run.
  \item Ran a controlled 2×2 ablation (original vs.\ pre-activation ResNet-56 × CIFAR-10 vs.\
        CIFAR-100) and found no measurable difference at depth 56 --- the gaps are smaller than the seed
        noise and point in opposite directions --- reported as a null result. 15 runs, \textasciitilde26 GPU-hours;
        write-up published at \href{https://resnet.amrabujabal.com}{resnet.amrabujabal.com}.
\end{cvbullets}

\cvproject{\href{https://github.com/AmroAbujabal/world-cup-2026-predictor}{World Cup 2026 Match Outcome Predictor}}{Full-Stack ML}{2026}
\begin{cvbullets}
